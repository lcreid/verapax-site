Our Services

  • Participatory and Collaborative Needs and Strengths Assessment to help inform decisions/planning of program or project
  • Evaluability Assessment (organization, program, project levels)
  • Key Performance Indicators (using SMART and SPICED approaches to setting goals and developing indicators)
  • Monitoring and Evaluation Matrix (organization, program, project levels)
  • Logic Model (program, project levels)
  • Evaluation Frameworks and data collection tools
  • Outcomes Harvesting/ Mapping Frameworks
  • Most Significant Change approach to evaluation
  • Metaevaluation
  • Developmental evaluation of pilot program or project
  • Assessment and training on organizational learning and mobilization of evaluation findings and recommendations.
