Life-Long Learning

Our evaluation practice is relational, contextualized, and consistent with the knowledges, expectations of people – especially those historically and systemically invisibilized -- in the communities we do evaluation with.

We acknowledge that mainstream evaluation approaches often marginalize both Indigenous-ancestral knowledge systems and evaluators original from the Global South or systemically disadvanta ged communities. We are a small consulting firm, but we are committed to deconstructing centuries of Western/European epistemological dominance over Indigenous knowledge systems. Our active membership in professional evaluation associations and relevant communities of practice, provides us continued space to deepen our knowledge and practice, while recognising and uplifting Indigenous paradigms.

We are active members of EvalIndigenous, a volunteer-driven international organization, formed in 2017, framed within the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples (2007), to ensure that evaluation policies and practices uphold the sovereign rights of Indigenous peoples. EvalIndigenous is one of the five networks of EvalPartners; a global movement to support the evaluation discipline to become a core instrument for social, economic and environmental transformation.

In line with our commitment to building evaluation capacity locally and globally, and upholding the rights and knowledge of Indigenous Peoples and people of Indigenous and African descent, Si nce 2024, we have been active members of the South African Monitoring & Evaluation Association (SAMEA); a voluntary organization of professional evaluation that promotes monitoring and evaluation as an important discipline and instrument for equitable and sustainable development in South Africa and beyond. SAMEA is recognized by the EvalYouth Global Network, as a Youth in Evaluation Super Champion. That’s why we collaborate with SAMEA’s Emerging Evaluators (EE) Programme, which nurtures the next generation of evaluators. Every year, we provide short-term paid internships and practicum opportunities that offer platforms for growth and meaningful contributions to the field for young and emergent evaluators in South Africa and the Americas.

Our consulting firm operates within the guidelines, standards and competences frameworks established by:

  • Canadian Evaluation Society Evaluation Competences.
  • South African Monitoring and Evaluation Association. Evaluation Competences and Framework.
  • Made in Africa Evaluation.
  • Red deSeguimiento, Evaluación y Sistematización de Latinoamerica y El Caribe (ReLAC).
  • EvalIndigenous Wolastock Declaration on Indigenous Evaluation.
  • Project Management Institute. The Standard for Project Management.
