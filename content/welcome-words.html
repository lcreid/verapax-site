Welcome Words

Welcome to the website of Verapax.

We are a purpose-driven consultancy firm owned and led by women of colour, committed to evaluation and knowledge mobilization that is rigorous, relevant, accessible, and impactful.

Verapax is a small boutique consultancy firm registered (2008) in Canada and Guatemala. We deliver evaluation and knowledge mobilization solutions locally and globally.

We operate at the intersection of theory, methods, ontology, epistemology, and ethics. That means, we possess proven credentials and expertise in theories that orient the public policies and initiatives for community development. We are committed to demonstrating rigour and credibility in our work; by gathering evidence using systematic approaches that acknowledge the complexity of social problems and their solutions. Our methodologies are relational, contextualized, and consistent with the knowledges, expectations of people – especially those historically and systemically invisibilized -- in the communities we do evaluation with.

Our mission as evaluators is to produce rigorous and credible evidence that serves three purposes: accountability, learning, and mobilize knowledge for equitable and sustainable public policies.

We specialize in collaborative, participatory, empowerment, and transformative approaches to equitable and sustainable systems change

Our evaluation services and approaches range from outcome harvesting and mapping to data storytelling, evaluation management systems, MEL (monitoring, evaluation, and learning) strategies, and capacity building to help organizations and communities collect and use data in ethical, effective, efficient, and impactful ways that meet their information and reporting needs.

Our director and principal consultant, team members and collaborators are community-based and possess relevant lived experiences that help them design and deliver strategies that acknowledge and promote the culture, values, and aspirations of the organizations and the communities we work with. We have extensive experience designing and implementing fit-for-context and fit-for-purpose strategies in fast-paced and high-stakes environments. We have subject matter expertise in gender-based social development, Indigenous knowledge systems; early childhood development; parenting support services; public health information and education; water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) systems; public legal information and education; entrepreneurship development; migration issues; and ecological systems.

We work with entities that embrace situated, participatory, collaborative and empowerment approaches to community or social development. Our clients include the non-profit/ social purpose sector, civil society organizations, public-private partnerships, the public sector, and academic and non-profit partnerships. To date, we have contributed our services to initiatives in diverse regions of Americas, Africa, and Asia.

If you are a social purpose organization passionate about designing and delivering initiatives that may contribute to addressing some of the systemic inequities that generate social problems such as poverty, violence, racism, and forced migration, we would love to connect with you.
